## Invitation as principal developer

Subject: Invitation to join the ASPECT Principal Developers

Dear [Name],

Every now and then, the ASPECT Principal Developers get together to talk about how the project is going and whether it's time to invite new people to join the team. This time, we all agreed that we'd love to invite you to become a Principal Developer!

You've been part of the ASPECT community for quite a while now, and your contributions - whether through code, discussions, or support for other users - have really helped move the project forward. As you already know, being a Principal Developer means helping review patches, answering user questions on the forum or at meetings in your area of expertise, and lending a hand with the general upkeep of the project (like releases, the website, documentation, workshops, and similar things).
Perhaps also to occasionally be introspective whether staying in that role is compatible with where you want to go with your career -- we do understand that the ability to commit time to the project ebbs and flows at times, but being and remaining a Principal Developer should come with a long-term commitment to the project even if, on a short time scale, you cannot devote much time.

If you'd like to talk about what the role involves or get some guidance, please don't hesitate to reach out to any of the current Principal Developers. And of course, if now doesn't feel like the right time, that's totally fine too - we truly value everything you've already done for ASPECT, and the opportunity will stay open for the future.

So, what do you think?

Warm regards,
[Your Name]
(on behalf of the ASPECT Principal Developers)
